# Privacy and submission scope

This repository is intended to contain only public scientific literature, original synthesis, a conceptual research proposal, and a mechanistic illustration.

**Do not upload:** patient case narratives, birth or medical history, variant-level patient findings, VCF/FASTQ/BAM files, clinical records, names, dates of birth, contact details, or confidential hackathon source data.

Before publishing, review the contents and document properties of all files and confirm any hackathon confidentiality and attribution requirements. Automated text scanning is not a substitute for a human privacy review.
